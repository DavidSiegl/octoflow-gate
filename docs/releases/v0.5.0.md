The first release of the gate as a program of its own, moved out of
[octoflow](https://github.com/DavidSiegl/octoflow-ai). Pin it by version and the sha256 in
`octoflow-gate-0.5.0.tar.gz.sha256`; the README's "Running the gate in a project" shows how.

- **`gate.toml`** holds the gate's settings: test-file patterns, golden directories, build-config files, linters,
  budgets and the commands that run the tests. Without one, a project is judged by the defaults.
- **The gate runs the tests itself**, with `gate.toml`'s `[[test]]` commands, and judges the JUnit and Cobertura
  reports they write. `--tests` and `--coverage` still take reports a CI has already written, in their place.
- **`quality.json`**, beside the quality file, is the report as data, versioned by `schema_version` and described
  by `schema/quality-v1.json`. Programs read it; the quality file's wording may change in any release.
- **The tarball** holds `gate.py`, the gate-rust helper built for Linux x86_64, the schema, golden quality files in
  both formats, and the README.
